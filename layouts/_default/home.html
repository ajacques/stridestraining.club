About Strides Training Club

Strides Training Club is athletic coaching built for real life. Off-the-shelf plans and rigid AI apps fail because they don't account for your work, family, or travel. We design human-first training around your actual schedule—turning training from an obligation into the freedom to live healthily.

Our Coaching Philosophy

  1. Human-First

    You are a person and an athlete. We design training to adapt to your life, stress, and family so fitness enhances your life instead of becoming a burden.

  2. Run Your Own Race

    Every workout has a purpose designed specifically for you. You'll know how each session equips you to run with perseverance the unique race marked out for your life.

  3. Progress, Not Perfection

    Missed a week? We don't judge—we adjust. By stripping away the weight of guilt, we adapt your plan to your current reality so one chaotic week never details your journey.

  4. You're Not Alone

    Stay supported with 1-on-1 coach check-ins and options for 1:1 workouts and local Seattle group sessions.
